Recheck Trigger Guide

Know when to recheck: before saving, before acting, and after waiting.

Recheck by trigger, not by promise

The sheet will not tell you when it has gone stale. Instead of trusting the snapshot, recheck on a concrete trigger — a moment when you are about to rely on the row.

Trigger 1 — Before saving a candidate

Reopen the page and confirm the fields just before you commit a row as a candidate. What you save should match what the page shows now, not what it showed when the row was first written.

Trigger 2 — Before an off-site action

Recheck just before you prepare to leave the site and act on the listing. The last thing you see should be the current page, not a remembered label.

Trigger 3 — After waiting

Reopen the page again after time has passed. Recheck what changed, and keep what is still open as a question.

Frame noteThese triggers are moments of action, not schedules. The point is to recheck when you are about to rely on the row.

A worked trigger
